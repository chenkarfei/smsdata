About SMS Data Ltd

SMS Data Ltd provides secure data intelligence and risk analytics tools for verified business customers.

Company Overview

A technology company built for responsible data access

SMS Data Ltd is a technology company focused on secure data intelligence, customer onboarding workflows, risk analytics, and responsible data access. Our platform helps approved businesses structure information, review risk signals, reduce fraud, and manage operational workflows through secure APIs and dashboards.

Because our products may be used in sensitive business processes, SMS Data Ltd may require verification of customers, authorised representatives, and account users before granting access.

What We Believe

  • Data intelligence tools should be secure, accountable, and used responsibly
  • Legitimate businesses deserve controlled access to practical risk tools
  • Verification and auditability protect customers and the platform alike
  • Clear workflows and transparent access policies build long-term trust
