What We Do

A more structured and secure way to work with data

SMS Data Ltd helps businesses work with onboarding, customer, and operational data in a more structured and secure way. Our platform supports approved customers with dashboards, APIs, workflow tools, and risk signals that help them review information, detect anomalies, reduce fraud, and maintain audit-ready records.

Access to our platform is restricted. Because our tools may be used in sensitive business processes, customers and authorised users may need to complete business verification, identity verification, sanctions screening, or additional account review before access is granted.
