1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how SMS Data Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies on our website and, where applicable, our platform. Cookies are small files placed on your device that help websites and services function, remember preferences, improve performance, and support security.

2. Types of Cookies We May Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for website functionality, security, authentication, account access, form submission, and fraud prevention. They cannot usually be disabled without affecting website or platform functionality.

Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website, identify technical issues, improve user experience, and measure performance.

Preference Cookies

These cookies may remember user preferences, such as language, region, or interface choices.

Security Cookies

These cookies may help us detect suspicious activity, protect accounts, prevent abuse, and secure our services.

3. Managing Cookies

You may be able to manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. If you disable certain cookies, some website or platform features may not work properly.

4. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
